About MindMate

MindMate is designed as a supportive wellbeing companion. It can help you reflect, organize your thoughts and find simple wellbeing activities.

MindMate is not a doctor, therapist or emergency service. For serious concerns, please talk to a trusted adult, qualified professional or appropriate emergency service.

You're not alone 🫶

If you're in immediate danger or feel unable to keep yourself safe, move to a safe place and tell a trusted adult right away. You can also contact your local emergency service.

A parent, guardian, teacher, school counselor, doctor or another trusted adult can help you get the right support.
