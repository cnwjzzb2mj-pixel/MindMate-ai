🌿 Calm Activities

Try one of these simple activities.

🌬️ Slow Breathing

Breathe in slowly for a few seconds, pause briefly, then breathe out slowly. Repeat several times.

👀 5 Things Around You

Notice five things you can see, four things you can touch, and three things you can hear.

📝 Brain Dump

Write down everything you're thinking about without worrying about spelling or making it perfect.

🚶 Take a Short Break

Step away from your screen for a few minutes, stretch gently, drink some water, or spend time somewhere comfortable.

🆘 Get Help

You don't have to handle everything alone.

If something feels too difficult, talk to someone you trust. This could be a parent, guardian, teacher, school counsellor, relative, or another trusted adult.

⚠️ If you feel unsafe

Tell a trusted adult immediately and ask them to stay with you while you get appropriate help. If there is an immediate danger, contact your local emergency service.

💙 Remember

Asking for help isn't a weakness. You deserve support, care and someone who will listen.
